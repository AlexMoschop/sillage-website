How We Operate

Fixed scope. Fixed price. Fragrance only.

We take one clear brief, the scent of your space, and stay inside it. We diagnose on site, write a prescription, present it in person, and come back to measure. Climate and music we assess and align to; we don't sell hardware or take a cut of what you install.

  • On-site diagnosis across every zone, including how airflow carries scent.
  • A written prescription you own, covering the direction, the placement, and the reasoning.
  • An in-person presentation with blotters, then a return visit to measure against the baseline.
