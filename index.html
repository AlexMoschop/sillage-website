About our method

Five steps. One prescription.

Every engagement follows the same diagnostic sequence, so the recommendation at the end is traceable to what we observed at the start.

Step 01

Primary evaluation

We walk the space room by room and read how scent currently carries through it — including via your HVAC and airflow.

Step 02

Time in the business

We spend working hours on site, watching customer patterns and asking staff who your clientele is today versus who you want.

Step 03

Analysis & matching

We match scent profiles to your target customer and business type, and check whether your music helps the scent or fights it.

Step 04

Presentation & testing

We present the shortlisted directions in person, blotters in hand, and trial them in the space before anything is committed.

Step 05

Return & refine

We come back, measure what changed against the baseline, and adjust — so the prescription ends up fitted, not guessed.

01 — On site

Strategy report

Stakeholder presentation

We walk you and your team through the report in person, with the blotters, and leave you the artefact.

Music

Music and scent must match energy

When the arousal level of the music matches the scent, evaluations, impulse purchasing and satisfaction all rise. When they clash, the environment reads as less pleasing than doing nothing.

Congruent

Slow ambient + lavender. Fast pop + citrus. Christmas music + pine.

Incongruent

Spa music + bright citrus. Dance music + lavender. Christmas music + summer fruit.

Mattila & Wirtz, 2001 — Journal of Retailing, 77(2) · Spangenberg et al., 2005 — Journal of Business Research, 58(11)

Climate

Comfort has a price attached

Comfortably warm rooms raised product valuation by roughly 10% over cool ones — while discomfort at either extreme pushes decisions off course. We observe climate and refer; we don't prescribe it.
